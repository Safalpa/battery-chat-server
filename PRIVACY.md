# Privacy Policy — 5% (Dying Battery Chat)

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

5% is an anonymous, ephemeral chat experiment. This policy explains what the
service knows about you — which is almost nothing, by design.

## What we collect

- **No accounts, no profiles, no contact information.** The server assigns you
  a random name (e.g. `SilentPanda42`) when you connect. The name exists only
  while your connection does, and rotates with every new conversation.
- **No message history.** Messages are relayed from one connected stranger to
  another through RAM and are never written to any database, log, or disk.
  When your connection ends, everything you said is gone.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies.**
- **No third-party data sharing.**

## What is processed, briefly

- Your messages pass through our server in memory so they can be delivered to
  the stranger you are currently paired with. This is momentary processing,
  not storage.
- The app reads your device's **battery level and charging state** on-device
  to enforce the 5% rule. This information never leaves your phone.
- An automated filter replaces phone-number-like sequences in messages before
  delivery, to discourage moving the conversation off the platform and to
  protect participants from unwanted contact.
- Screenshots of the conversation are blocked by the app while a chat is on
  screen. No technical measure is perfect — only share what you would accept
  a stranger reading.

## What we expect from you

- Be 18 or older. The service is not directed at children.
- Do not harass, threaten, or attempt to identify other participants.
- Do not use the service for illegal content.
- Anyone you talk to is an anonymous stranger. Use the same judgment you would
  with any stranger on the internet.

## Data retention

Nothing survives your session. Server restarts erase everything instantly.
There is no backup, because there is no data.
